Gin Rummy

You 0Opponent 0First to 100

Two players, two browsers. One of you creates a game and sends the link, and the other opens it. Moves pass through a small relay that keeps nothing, and the game itself lives in the creator's browser.

Send this link to your opponent. The game starts when they open it.

Your hand

Ten cards each. On your turn, draw from the stock or take the top discard, then discard one card. Melds are three or four of a kind, or three or more in a row of one suit (aces low). Knock when your unmatched cards, the deadwood, total 10 or less, counting aces as 1 and face cards as 10. Gin is zero deadwood and scores 25 plus the other player's deadwood. After a knock the other player lays off cards onto your melds. If they end with as much deadwood or less, they undercut you and score the difference plus 25. A hand ends with no score if the stock runs down to two cards.

The game lives in the creator's browser tab. If they close it the game ends, and if the other player reloads the link they rejoin where they were.
