LiDAR-inertial odometry

Why onboard

SLAM runs entirely on the Jetson Orin NX 16GB. If the radio drops, the drone still knows where it is, so flight never depends on the ground station.

Why LiDAR-inertial

The IMU carries the pose between scans and the scan matcher corrects its drift. Four candidates are on the list; I haven't picked one yet.

Sensors and compute

Pandar40P

40 channels, about 200 m range, about 910 g. The airframe and battery have to be sized around that mass.

Two LiDARs on one Orin NX

At the edge of feasible. I'll benchmark it before committing to the second unit.

Extrinsics first

LiDAR-to-IMU extrinsics get calibrated before any SLAM run. A bad transform here shows up later as a smeared map.

Stitching

Frontier exploration

Each drone heads for the edge of what is already mapped, so the swarm covers new ground instead of retracing it.

Compressed fragments over mesh radio

Drones send map fragments to the ground station. It merges them for display only; no drone waits on it.

Outputs

LAS/LAZ point cloud, 3D mesh, occupancy grid, PDF report.
